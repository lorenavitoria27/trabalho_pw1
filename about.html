About Alternative Therapies

Alternative therapy is a bunch of healthcare practices that work with traditional medic treatments to bring physical and emotional well-being. So, instead of using conventional methods like conversations with psychologists or medicine use, alternative therapies is known for its natural practices, like teas, meditation, energetic alignment and other methods. The purpose of it all is that the patient will not be only treated from its illness symptoms, but completely, from inside out.
